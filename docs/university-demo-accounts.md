## Dataset

The complete 136-account roster includes 100 Moratuwa students, 20 Moratuwa lecturers, 12 additional SLIIT students, three SLIIT lecturers, and one platform administrator. The shared student is included in Moratuwa’s 100 students and is not counted as an additional SLIIT student. All Moratuwa students have approved university, faculty, and department memberships. Some also administer clubs. A further 24 membership applications are pending; the dataset also includes rejected and suspended club requests.

The roster includes 40 Moratuwa and six SLIIT resources, 920 resource slots, 230 bookings with completed/cancelled/upcoming history, 12 Moratuwa disputes, point ledger entries, ratings, notifications, and terminal notification-delivery examples. Seeding does not send email or push notifications.
